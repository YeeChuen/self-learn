Here is the updated list, moving Post Search into the Search & Discovery category while keeping the other items in their most common interview groupings.
## 1. Application System Design (User-Facing Products)
These questions focus on the end-to-end user experience, product flow, and major architectural trade-offs.
    * Social & Content Feeds: Focus on fan-out, feed generation, and high-read volume.
        * Examples: Twitter, Facebook, Instagram.
    * Transactional & Booking: Focus on ACID compliance, distributed locking, and preventing double-booking.
        * Examples: Ticketmaster, Hotel Booking, Parking Lot, Robinhood(Consumer trading)
    * Real-Time Communications: Focus on low-latency, persistent connections (WebSockets), and message delivery.
        * Examples: WhatsApp, Slack, Tinder(Match&Chat logic), Trading View(Charts)
    * Marketplace & Logistics: Focus on real-time matching, location tracking, and ETA calculations.
        * Examples: Uber, DoorDash, Tinder(Discovery&Swipe logic).
    * Streaming & Storage: Focus on high-bandwidth binary data, global delivery (CDNs), and library synchronization.
        * Examples: Netflix, Spotify, Google Drive, Dropbox.
    * Utility Applications: Standalone services providing specific user utilities.
        * Example: Bit.ly(URL Shortener).

## 2. Collaborative & Productivity Systems
This category addresses the unique challenges of real-time state synchronization and isolated execution environments.
    * Collaborative Editing: Focus on conflict resolution (OT or CRDTs) and low-latency state syncing.
        * Example: Google Docs.
    * Online Judges & Execution: Focus on secure, isolated code execution (Sandboxing) and high-concurrency leaderboards.
        * Example: LeetCode.

## 3. Backend & Data System Design (Internal Logic)
These focus on how data is indexed, searched, and distributed behind the scenes.
    * Search & Discovery: Focus on high-speed indexing, ranking, and retrieval.
        * Examples: Google Search, Post Search, Autocomplete(Type-ahead).
    * Analytics & Event Ingestion: Focus on massive write throughput and stream processing.
        * Examples: Ad Click Aggregator, Real-time Dashboards.
    * Payment & Financial: Focus on idempotency, ledger integrity, and payment gateways.
        * Examples: Stripe API, Digital Wallets.
    * Crawler Systems: Focus on graph traversal and scheduling.
        * Example: Web Crawler (Google Bot).

## 4. Infrastructure System Design (The Building Blocks)
These are specialized middleware components used to protect, scale, or decouple services.
    * Access & Traffic Control: Focus on security, reliability, and protecting internal services.
        * Examples: Rate Limiter, API Gateway, Load Balancer.
    * Data & Performance: Focus on latency reduction and data consistency models.
        * Examples: Distributed Cache (Redis), Key-Value Store (Dynamo).
    * Messaging & Notification: Focus on decoupling services and asynchronous delivery.
        * Examples: Notification System, Message Queues (Kafka).
    * Naming & Routing: Focus on coordination and discovery across a distributed system.
        * Examples: Unique ID Generator (Snowflake), Service Discovery.
